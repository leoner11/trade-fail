# Company FAQ

## Who we are
We are a manufacturer and exporter of power equipment: silent diesel
generator sets, open-type generator sets, automatic transfer switch (ATS)
panels, and soundproof containers. All products are factory-direct.

## Certifications
Our generator products carry CE and ISO9001 certification.

## Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
MOQ depends on the product — it is listed on each product sheet. Accessory
items such as ATS panels have a small MOQ; generators start from 1 unit.

## Pricing
Prices depend on quantity, configuration and destination. Ask our sales
assistant for an indicative price on standard catalog items; formal
quotations (PDF) are prepared by the sales team.

## Custom / OEM / ODM requests
We support OEM and ODM work: custom branding, modified specifications,
special voltages. These requests go to our engineering team for review and
a tailored quotation.

## Payment terms
Payment terms are discussed case by case with our sales team depending on
order size and destination country.

## Shipping and lead time
Shipping arrangements (incoterms, freight, lead time) are confirmed with
our sales team for each order.

## After-sales
If you have any problem with an order or a delivered unit, please tell us
what happened — your message will be handled by our team as a priority.
