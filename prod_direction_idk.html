Why sprints exist

Not every client is ready for a ¥150K conversation. Not every problem needs a full coordination layer. Sprints unbundle the 15 functions into individually deliverable modules — a pricing algorithm, an ERP gateway, a workflow engine — scoped and delivered in days, not months. Each sprint is a paid proof of MJCX's speed and architecture quality. Clients who want more depth after a sprint are pre-qualified for the diagnostic. Cash comes in while the enterprise pipeline develops.

Two-Contract Structure

Usage access is revocable. Ownership transfers only on full payment.

Contract 1: Usage License

软件使用许可协议
  • Client gets access to use the deployed system
  • Revocable on non-payment — kill switch active
  • Monthly or milestone-based payment schedule
  • MJCX retains all intellectual property
  • Client data remains client's property
  • System degrades to read-only on missed payment, not hard crash
  • Data export within 30 days of termination

Contract 2: Ownership Transfer

软件著作权转让协议
  • Executes ONLY upon completion of all payments
  • Transfers registered software copyright to client
  • Source code delivery included
  • References specific 软件著作权登记 (copyright registration)
  • Standard pricing = ownership cost
  • Flexible premium = access fee on top of ownership cost
  • Registration under 铭捷软件 supports HNTE credential path

Kill Switch — Standard Infrastructure

Built into every deployment from day one, not bolted on after. Same license management system across trial agents and custom builds.

Trial Agents
Auto-lock at day 30. System continues in read-only mode — all data preserved, no new queries.
Subscriptions
Missed payment → 7-day grace → read-only. Resume on payment. Subscription via credit card auto-charge.
Custom Builds
Remote license validation. Missed installment → graceful degradation. Full lock after 30 days. Ownership transfer blocked.
